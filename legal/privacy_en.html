Privacy Policy

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyBizzCard (the “App”) accesses, uses, processes, and retains information. References to “we,” “us,” and “our” mean the developer of MyBizzCard identified in its Google Play listing.

Privacy and support contact: mybizzzcard@gmail.com.

---------------------------------------
1. Information handled by the App
---------------------------------------
MyBizzCard helps you scan business cards and manage contact information. Information you scan or enter can include names, job titles, company names, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, addresses, card images, and local-language contact details. You can also add batch names, CRM notes, tags, and reminder information.

Scanned results and your contact-management records are stored on your device. They may also be included in device backups or transferred when you use export, sharing, phonebook, or other device/account features. AI processing and technical service records are described separately below.

You do not need to create a MyBizzCard user account.

---------------------------------------
2. On-device and offline features
---------------------------------------
Offline card recognition, QR scanning, manual entry, cropping, and local contact management process their card content on your device. Those actions do not send card content to OpenAI unless you separately use an AI feature.

Some scanning components use Google services and may download required components or communicate with Google as part of providing the feature. Their availability and handling of service information are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy information.

---------------------------------------
3. AI features and processing
---------------------------------------
When you use an AI extraction or translation feature, the selected business card image or OCR text is transmitted over HTTPS through our backend, hosted on Render, to the OpenAI API. The extracted contact fields are returned to the App.

The submitted content can include personal information visible on the selected card, such as names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, and addresses.

AI scanning does not require uploading your existing phonebook. Phonebook access for destination-account selection and duplicate checking is handled separately on your device.

Our backend does not retain submitted card images, OCR text, or extracted contact contents after processing is completed. Separate technical records are described in Section 13. OpenAI's processing and retention are subject to the applicable API terms and our account's data controls. See OpenAI's API data controls (https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

AI usage limits depend on your plan and are shown in the App. You can use available non-AI features without submitting card content to OpenAI.

---------------------------------------
4. Batch lists, CRM, and reminders
---------------------------------------
You can organize scanned contacts into lists and add notes, tags, and follow-up details. These records are maintained locally unless you choose to export or share them, save them to another app or account, or include them in device backups.

Where a reminder feature uses your device calendar, the App requests the calendar permissions needed for that feature. Calendar entries saved to a synchronized account may be processed by that account's provider under its settings and privacy policy.

We do not maintain your batch CRM records on our processing server.

---------------------------------------
5. Responsible use of contact information
---------------------------------------
Business card and contact details may belong to other people. Use the App only where you have an appropriate legal basis to scan, save, export, contact, or share those details. Obtain permission and provide notices where required by applicable law, and respect requests concerning those details.

You control which cards you submit for AI processing and which contacts you save, export, or share. These responsibilities do not exclude or limit any obligations we have under applicable privacy law.

---------------------------------------
6. Phonebook access and contact saving
---------------------------------------
When you choose Save to Phonebook, the App requests contacts permissions where required by Android.

- Read Contacts: used to identify available destination accounts and, in versions with duplicate checking, compare selected contacts against existing phonebook entries.
- Write Contacts: used to save the selected contact information to the destination account you choose.

Phonebook information read for these features is processed locally on your device. These features do not upload your existing phonebook contacts to our backend or OpenAI.

Saved details may include names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, addresses, and applicable notes, tags, batch details, and reminder information.

If you select Google, Samsung, Exchange, or another synchronized account, its provider may synchronize saved contacts according to your account settings and its privacy policy.

You can decline or revoke contacts permissions in Android settings. Features requiring those permissions will be unavailable, but other App features remain accessible. Manage or delete contacts already saved to your phonebook using your contacts app or account provider.

---------------------------------------
7. Exports, backups, and external storage
---------------------------------------
CSV, Excel, VCF, and backup files can contain contact information, card images, and other records you choose to include. These files are generated on your device. Our backend does not receive these user export or backup files through the export feature.

Files may remain in shared storage after uninstalling the App. If you copy them to cloud storage or another device, the destination provider's settings and privacy policy apply. Android or device-provider backup services may also back up eligible app data, depending on your device settings and the App's backup configuration.

---------------------------------------
8. Email, messaging, and sharing
---------------------------------------
When you choose an email or sharing action, the App passes the selected information to the app you choose so you can review and complete the action. Depending on the feature, that information can include recipient addresses, message text, contact details, images, or exported files.

Customer messages are handled by your selected messaging or email app and provider, not sent by our backend on your behalf. Our backend may separately send technical service-alert emails to the developer. Those alerts are designed not to contain card contents, phonebook contents, or purchase tokens.

---------------------------------------
9. Advertising
---------------------------------------
The current App does not display third-party advertisements or integrate an advertising network. We do not provide card contents or phonebook contents to advertising partners.

The App includes Google Analytics for Firebase, which can process app-instance, device, and advertising identifiers depending on your settings, consent choices, and the applicable SDK configuration. Having analytics or an advertising identifier permission does not mean the App displays advertisements. Analytics is described in Section 11.

We will update this policy if our advertising practices change.

---------------------------------------
10. Payments and subscriptions
---------------------------------------
Google Play handles subscription purchases and payments. We do not receive or store your payment-card details.

The App and our backend use subscription purchase tokens, subscription product information, and app information to verify entitlement with Google Play and apply the relevant usage limits. Subscription verification results may be temporarily cached.

Pricing, renewal terms, and subscription management are provided through Google Play. Manage or cancel your subscription through Google Play; uninstalling the App does not cancel it.

---------------------------------------
11. Analytics, diagnostics, and technical logs
---------------------------------------
We use Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Play reporting, and backend service logs to understand App usage, investigate failures, and measure reliability and processing performance.

These services can process technical information such as app-instance or installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, usage events, crash traces, request identifiers, scan counts, processing times, image byte sizes, and error information. Some information may be linked to a pseudonymous identifier; we do not describe all diagnostic information as anonymous.

Phonebook contents and scanned card contents are not intended to be included in analytics events or routine performance logs. The App's backend access logs mask client IP addresses, but network and infrastructure providers may independently process connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure their services.

See Firebase privacy information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---------------------------------------
12. Security and service providers
---------------------------------------
AI requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. We use service providers, including Render for backend hosting, OpenAI for AI processing, Google/Firebase for the services described above, and our email provider for developer service alerts. They process the information needed to provide their respective services under the applicable terms and privacy arrangements.

Locally saved information is subject to your device's security and backup settings. Exports, shared content, and synchronized phonebook or calendar entries are also subject to the security and policies of the destinations you choose. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their applicable terms and data-protection arrangements govern that processing alongside our obligations under applicable law.

---------------------------------------
13. Data retention
---------------------------------------
We do not retain business card images, extracted card text, CRM notes, tags, reminders, or user-exported files on our processing server after processing is completed.

Our backend retains technical records, including hashed device identifiers, scan usage counters, request identifiers, and timestamps, to enforce usage limits, prevent duplicate processing, and troubleshoot service issues. These records are separate from card contents. Usage and quota records currently have no automatic deletion schedule. Duplicate-request records are removed during subsequent cleanup after their checking window expires. Backend logs follow the hosting provider's applicable retention settings.

OpenAI and Google/Firebase retention depends on the service, its applicable policies, and the account settings or controls in use. Our backend's non-retention of card content is not a promise that every service provider immediately deletes all information.

Local app data remains until you delete it or remove the App's local storage. Uninstalling does not necessarily delete exported files, saved phonebook contacts, calendar entries, or device/cloud backups. Manage those copies through the relevant device, app, or account provider.
